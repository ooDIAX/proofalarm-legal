What ProofAlarm does
with your information

Effective September 11, 2026

The short version

ProofAlarm has no accounts, no login, and no email collection. Your alarms, mission settings, and any photo taken for a wake-up mission never leave your device. We only collect anonymous usage analytics and crash reports to improve the app, and Apple handles all payment processing directly — we never see your card details.

01 Information we do not collect

  • We do not require or offer account creation or login of any kind.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, or photo library — except the momentary camera use described below when you actively use the Object Hunt mission.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

02 Information processed on your device only

Alarms and settings. Alarm times, repeat schedules, chosen wake-up missions, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Camera (Object Hunt mission). If you choose the Object Hunt mission, the app uses your camera to verify you've photographed a specific object. The image is analyzed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework — it is never uploaded, never leaves your device, and is not saved once the check completes.

Streaks and insights. Your wake-up streak and any in-app statistics are calculated and stored locally on your device.

03 Information we do collect

Anonymous usage analytics. We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people use the app — for example, which onboarding screen someone reaches, or which answer they pick to a lifestyle question during setup. This is tied to a random device identifier, never to your name or any personal identifier, and is not linked across other apps or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Crash and diagnostic data. If the app crashes or errors, technical diagnostic information (a stack trace, device model) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. This never includes your alarms, photos, or any personal content.

Subscription status. ProofAlarm's subscription is billed and processed entirely through Apple's App Store — we never see or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, to check whether your Apple ID has an active subscription; RevenueCat assigns your device an anonymous identifier for this purpose and receives your App Store transaction receipt to validate it. RevenueCat never receives your name, email, or payment details from us.

04 Third-party services we use

  • Apple (App Store, StoreKit, AlarmKit) — handles subscription billing and the alarm-ringing system itself. Governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google) — Analytics and Crashlytics only, as described above. Governed by Google's Firebase privacy terms.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status validation, as described above. Governed by RevenueCat's own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third party for their own marketing purposes.

05 Children's privacy

ProofAlarm is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal identifiers from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06 Your choices

  • Core alarm functionality does not require an internet connection.
  • Deleting the app permanently removes all locally stored alarms, settings, and streak data from your device.
  • ProofAlarm does not currently have an in-app toggle to individually opt out of anonymous analytics. If you'd like this added, or have any privacy question, contact us below.

07 Data retention

Locally stored data (alarms, settings, streaks) stays on your device until you delete it or delete the app. Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase per Google's standard retention periods, currently up to 14 months.

08 Changes to this policy

If we materially change how ProofAlarm handles your information, we'll update this page and its effective date above.
