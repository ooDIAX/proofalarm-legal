01 The service

ProofAlarm is an alarm clock app that requires you to complete a short wake-up mission — a math problem, a photo challenge, a memory pattern, or typing a quote — to turn off an alarm. Tapping the alert's on-screen buttons routes you into that mission.

Using your device's hardware buttons to force-close the app or power off the device is a known platform limitation outside ProofAlarm's control, not something the app can prevent — see Section 4.

02 Subscriptions
